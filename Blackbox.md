# Always remember metacognition is the key , you should know where your thoughts are going.Drive them properly.

## Phase 1 
- Check the constraints, Find your upper bound of algorithm  for the problem. 
- Transform the texts to Mathematical block. Understand what is given and what is really asked. 
- Understand the rules of the game. What you can do and what not. 
- Understand what is asked , and what are the condition to achieve that , if its asking for maximum sum , find when a sum can be maximum. 
- Work with examples to extract insights and invariants. 
## Phase 2 
- Generate idea according to the insights you got . 
- work with the type of the problem , find if the search space is monotonic or dynamic ?
- whats the minimum that you have to do. 
- Find if its a dp problem or not. 
- Dont try to simulate a problem if it doesnt require you to , by just thinking about whats the minimum information i need to know to take a decision . 
## Phase 3 
- Never trust your assumption (talking from personal experience)
- prune your ideas with worst testcases within the rules of the game.
- Find for what testcase your logic will fail horribly 
## phase 4 
- Explain your model and pseudocode shortly for better implementation. so you know what you are doing to implement what part of the model 
- Run the samples. 
- Run some edge cases
